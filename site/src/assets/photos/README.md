# Put your church's photographs here

The build imports these by filename, so the NAMES matter. Replace each with a
photo of your own church at the same rough shape, keep the name, and the site
picks it up with no code change.

| filename | what it is | where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `sanctuary.jpg` | the room, wide, empty or nearly | Home hero |
| `welcome.jpg` | someone being greeted at the door | I'm New |
| `worship.jpg` | congregation standing and singing | Home, About |
| `preaching-hand.jpg` | a hand on the Bible at the pulpit | Sermons |
| `prayer.jpg` | people praying together | Give, Prayer |
| `talking.jpg` | people talking after a service | About |
| `hands.jpg` | hands, close — held or open | About, Prayer |
| `hymnal.jpg` | an open hymnal, close | Music, Bulletin |
| `bowed.jpg` | heads bowed in a service | About |
| `exterior.jpg` | the building from outside | Contact |
| `exterior-wide.jpg` | the building, wide, with sky | Contact hero |
| `kids-pews.jpg` | the children's ministry | Kids page |
| `youth-row.jpg` | teenagers in a service | Youth page |
| `ladies-talking.jpg` | women of the church talking | Ladies page |
| `men-men.jpg` | men of the church together | Men page |
| `seniors-conversation.jpg` | older members talking | Seniors page |
| `preacher.jpg` | a second preacher or guest speaker | About, staff |

The build imports these **by these exact filenames**. Rename one and the build
breaks; replace one and nothing else has to change.

## What is in here now

Stand-in photographs, generated rather than photographed, so you can see the
design working before your own pictures exist. **They are not your church and
they are not anyone's church.** Replace every one before you launch — a visitor
who reverse-image-searches a stock face on a church website has learned
something you did not mean to tell them.

Any still showing a grey cross are ones no stand-in was made for.

**Shoot or crop these around 2400px wide.** Astro generates every smaller size
it needs; anything larger just makes the repo big. A 6000px original is 10MB to
serve 2400px of it.

**Get consent before you publish a face**, and get it from a parent for a child.
This is the single thing on the whole site most likely to cause hurt, and it is
not a technical problem — no amount of code makes it safe to publish a photo of
somebody's child without asking.
