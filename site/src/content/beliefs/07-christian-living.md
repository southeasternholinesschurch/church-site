title: "About Christian Living"
order: 7
---

We believe that followers of Jesus are called to live differently from the world — not out
of pride, but in love and obedience. We aim to reflect Christ in our words, our actions,
and our attitudes. As citizens, we strive to be respectful and obedient to government,
unless it contradicts God's Word. Our lives should be living testimonies of God's grace.
